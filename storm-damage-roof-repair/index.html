How we handle storm-damaged roofs

A storm does not always leave an obvious hole. Hail bruises and wind-lifted shingles still matter, and they are easier to document sooner rather than later.

Storm inspection

We look at shingles, flashing, valleys, and penetrations for hail marks, wind lift, and openings after a storm.

Insurance documentation

We help you document the damage and work through the claim for a repair or replacement. Approval is up to your insurer.

Hail damage repair

When hail has bruised or broken shingles, we repair or replace what is needed so the roof can shed water again.

Wind damage repair

Blown shingles and lifted edges get fixed before the next rain finds its way inside.

Emergency tarping

If the roof is already open, we can put a tarp on it to help protect the house until permanent work is done.

Full replacement when needed

If the storm damage is widespread, we can replace the roof after the claim and inspection process.

What to do after wind or hail

Stay off a damaged roof. If water is getting in, call us about tarping. Then schedule a free inspection so the damage is documented while it is still fresh. We can help you work with your insurance company on the next steps — we do not promise a claim will be approved.

Serving Ringgold, Fort Oglethorpe, Rossville, Chickamauga, Dalton, and Chattanooga. Call (423) 290-0862 if the roof is open right now.

A simple order of operations

  • Protect people first, and stay off the roof.
  • Call for a tarp if water is entering the house.
  • Get the damage inspected and photographed.
  • Work the insurance claim, then complete the repair or replacement.

Storm and hail damage FAQs

Practical answers after wind or hail hits the roof.

Should I wait to inspect the roof after a storm?

It is better not to wait. Documenting damage sooner helps you understand what needs repair and gives you a clearer record for insurance.

Do you help with the insurance claim?

Yes. We inspect the roof, document what we find, and help you work through the claim for repairs or a replacement. We cannot guarantee that a claim will be approved or that a particular amount will be paid.

What if the roof is leaking right now?

Call (423) 290-0862. We provide emergency tarping to help stop the water, then we can plan the lasting repair. See our emergency roof repair page.

Is the storm inspection free?

Yes. The roof inspection is free and comes with no obligation to hire us for the repair.

Where do you do storm-damage work?

We serve Ringgold, Fort Oglethorpe, Rossville, Chickamauga, Dalton, Chattanooga, and nearby communities in North Georgia and Hamilton County, Tennessee.

What warranty do you offer after the repair or replacement?
