Emergency roof repair FAQs

What to expect when the roof is leaking or open.

How fast can you get here?

We treat emergency calls as a priority and work to arrive as quickly as we can. Response time depends on when you call, the weather, and how far the property is from Ringgold. Call (423) 290-0862 and we will tell you how soon we can get there.

Are you available 24/7?

We do not claim around-the-clock coverage. Call us and we will be honest about how soon we can respond.

What should I do until you arrive?

Stay off the roof. If you can do so safely, move belongings away from the drip and use buckets to catch water. Do not try to walk a wet or damaged roof yourself.

Will you repair the roof the same day?

Often the first visit is to tarp the roof and stop the water. Lasting repairs or a replacement are scheduled once we can inspect the damage and, if needed, work through insurance.

Where do you provide emergency roofing?

We serve Ringgold, Fort Oglethorpe, Rossville, Chickamauga, Dalton, Chattanooga, and nearby towns in North Georgia and Hamilton County, Tennessee.

How do I request help if I cannot call right now?

Use the form on this page with the property address. If the leak is active, calling is still the faster option.
